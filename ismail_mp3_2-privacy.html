Privacy Policy for Mp3 Music Downloader app

Last updated: December 15, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Mp3 Music Downloader app ("we", "us", "our", or "the app"), developed by isoappsdev, collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Android application available on Google Play Store.

By using Mp3 Music Downloader app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Music Library Data: Information about songs you download, playlists you create, and your music preferences within the app
  • Search Queries: Keywords and search terms you use to find music

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, Android version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, interaction patterns, crash reports, and performance data
  • Network Information: Internet connection type and status
  • Analytics Data: App usage statistics collected through Firebase Analytics

2.3 Permissions

Our app requests the following permissions:

  • Internet Access: To search and download music
  • Notification Access: To show music playback controls and download progress
  • Foreground Service: To play music in the background
  • Wake Lock: To keep the device awake during music playback
  • Media Content Access: To access and manage downloaded music files

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide music search and download functionality
  • Enable music playback and playlist management
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Display relevant advertisements through AppLovin MAX
  • Comply with legal requirements

4. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with the following third-party services:

4.1 Firebase (Google)

  • Firebase Analytics: For app usage analytics and crash reporting
  • Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4.2 AppLovin MAX

  • Purpose: To display advertisements within the app
  • Data Collected: Device identifiers, IP address, app usage data
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/

4.3 Music Search APIs

  • We use third-party APIs to search for music content
  • Search queries are sent to these services to retrieve music information
  • We do not store or share your personal information with these services

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Downloaded music files are stored locally on your device using Android's MediaStore API
  • Playlist and preference data are stored in your device's local storage
  • We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your information
  • No personal data is transmitted to our servers

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share aggregated, non-personally identifiable information with:

  • Analytics providers to improve app functionality
  • Advertising partners (AppLovin) to display relevant ads
  • Legal authorities when required by law

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete that information.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You can:

  • Delete downloaded music files directly from the app or your device's file manager
  • Clear app data through your device settings to remove all stored information
  • Opt out of personalized ads through your device's advertising settings
  • Disable analytics collection through Firebase settings

9. Data Retention

  • Downloaded music files remain on your device until you delete them
  • App usage analytics are retained according to Firebase's data retention policies
  • Advertising data is retained according to AppLovin's policies

10. International Data Transfers

The third-party services we use may transfer data internationally. By using our app, you consent to the transfer of information to countries outside of your country of residence.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
